YouTube Pairwise Video Labeling

In this task you will be presented with pairs of YouTube videos. You will be asked the question "which video is more right leaning?" or "which video is more left leaning?" In other words, which video expresses viewpoints or associations that are more congenial to the right or left wing of the political spectrum?

You do not have to watch the video if you feel confident in your answer judging solely by the title, channel, and thumbnail. Don't spend too much time per pair. If you are unsure, go with your best guess.

If one or both videos are unavailable, try clicking the "watch on YouTube" button. If they still are unavailable, click the "video is unavailable" button.
